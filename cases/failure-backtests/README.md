# Failure / False-Positive Backtest Library

## Purpose

A strong opportunity framework must be able to explain not only why a category succeeded, but also why a seemingly compelling opportunity **did not produce durable economics**.

These cases are deliberately selected to attack the framework:

- strong attention but weak retention;
- strong funding but weak payment;
- strong technology but poor consumer behavior;
- rapid capital formation followed by consolidation;
- a good category with a bad entry point;
- an apparently open window that closed before a small operator could establish power.

## Rules

1. Judge the opportunity using information that was reasonably observable **at the time**.
2. Do not use hindsight as if it were a contemporaneous signal.
3. Separate category failure from company failure.
4. Record capital heat separately from customer pull.
5. Identify the earliest counter-signal that should have changed the decision.
6. State what the framework could not have known.
7. Do not treat a failed category as proof that the underlying need was fake.

## Current Backtests

- `consumer-3d-printing.md` — technology excitement outran consumer workflow and repeat-use economics.
- `social-audio.md` — extraordinary attention and venture heat did not establish durable consumer retention or monetization at the expected scale.
- `consumer-metaverse.md` — capital and narrative heat exceeded evidence for persistent consumer behavior and durable willingness to pay.
- `daily-deals.md` — real consumer demand existed, but weak merchant economics, discount dependency and low switching costs made the business structurally difficult to defend.
- `google-glass-consumer.md` — a powerful technology signal did not imply a viable mass-market consumer wedge at that moment.

These are **synthetic analytical reconstructions**, not investment advice and not claims that every company in a category failed.

## Required Backtest Template

For every new case:

`Contemporaneous thesis → Capital heat → Payment evidence → Industry formation → Operator opportunity → Power Pool → Window → Counter-signals → Earliest kill signal → What remained unknowable → Verdict`
