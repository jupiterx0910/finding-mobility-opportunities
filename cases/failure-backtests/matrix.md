# Failure Backtest Matrix — Capital Heat × Payment × Retention

This matrix is the anti-hype layer of Opportunity Radar.

Its purpose is not to say that venture capital is good or bad. It asks whether **capital belief, customer payment and repeat behavior agree with each other**.

## Core Matrix

| Capital Heat | Payment | Retention / Recurrence | Interpretation | Default Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | Low | Unknown/Weak | Weak thesis | REJECT / WATCH |
| Low | Strong | Strong | **Underfollowed opportunity** | BUY A REAL OPTION / START if operator fit is strong |
| High | Weak | Weak | **Hype divergence** | WATCH / REJECT |
| High | Strong | Weak | **Fragile growth** | BUY A REAL OPTION only; test retention |
| High | Strong | Strong | **Category formation** | Evaluate Power Pool, competition and operator fit |
| Falling | Strong | Strong | **Post-hype contrarian opening** | Re-check pricing, competition and access |
| Falling | Weak | Weak | **Unwinding** | REJECT unless a new structural change appears |

Capital Heat never overrides a missing payer, poor recurrence or inaccessible Power Pool.

## The Three Divergences

### 1. Hype Divergence

`Capital ↑↑ + attention ↑↑ + payment weak + retention weak`

Typical mistake: interpreting investor conviction as customer proof.

### 2. Subsidy Divergence

`Payment exists + usage grows + contribution economics worsen`

Typical mistake: treating revenue as proof that each transaction creates value.

MoviePass is the cleanest case in this library.

### 3. Recurrence Divergence

`First payment strong + repeat behavior weak / expensive`

Typical mistake: treating customer acquisition as product-market fit.

## Required Agent Check

Before a hype-sensitive opportunity can receive **START**, the agent must answer:

1. Is payment direct or subsidized?
2. Is retention / recurrence independently observed?
3. Does contribution economics improve, stay flat or worsen with scale?
4. Is capital financing customer value creation or masking weak economics?
5. If external funding stopped for 12 months, could the business still serve customers?
6. Does capital heat increase competition faster than it improves the operator's access?

If questions 1–4 are unresolved, **START is not permitted**.

## Decision Rule

> Capital heat changes the environment. It does not prove the opportunity.

The strongest positive configuration for many small operators is often:

`real payment + real recurrence + modest capital heat + unresolved bottleneck + operator edge`.

The strongest warning configuration is:

`C4 Euphoric + weak payment/retention + rising competition + no owned Power Pool`.
