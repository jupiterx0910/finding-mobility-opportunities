# Backtest — Shared E-Scooters / Bird

> Analytical reconstruction. Shared micromobility generated real rides and revenue; the false positive was assuming strong usage automatically implied attractive fleet economics and durable operator power.

## 1. Contemporaneous Thesis

The thesis was:

`cheap connected scooters + app distribution + dense city demand → high-frequency short trips → scalable urban mobility network`

The usage signal was real.

## 2. Capital Heat

**Capital Heat:** C3–C4 during the category's expansion phase.

Large venture funding and later public-market access supported rapid fleet deployment and geographic expansion.

## 3. Payment Evidence

Strong.

Customers paid for rides, and the category reached substantial scale. Bird reported more than 95 million rides by 2021 and later reported hundreds of millions of dollars in annual revenue.

## 4. Retention / Recurrence

Repeat urban mobility demand existed, but recurrence alone was insufficient because each ride depended on physical assets, charging/rebalancing, city permissions, maintenance and vehicle life.

## 5. Industry Formation

Strong enough to create dedicated operators, city regulation, fleet hardware, charging/operations workflows and competitors.

## 6. Operator Opportunity

The generic “deploy scooters everywhere” thesis was capital intensive.

Potentially stronger wedges:

- fleet maintenance;
- battery / charging infrastructure;
- municipal compliance tools;
- parking / docking;
- B2B campuses and controlled environments;
- fleet software.

## 7. Power Pool

Cities controlled permits and public-space access. Hardware durability, utilization, operations and financing heavily influenced margins. Consumer demand did not imply operator pricing power.

## 8. Window

**Window:** Opening → Hot/Crowding → Consolidating / Unwinding.

## 9. Counter-Signals

- high fleet capex;
- depreciation and asset impairment;
- regulatory dependence city by city;
- recurring losses and negative operating cash flow;
- expansion requiring continued financing.

Bird's 2022 results included significant asset impairment and a going-concern warning.

## 10. Earliest Kill Signal

If mature cohorts cannot generate attractive vehicle-level economics after depreciation, maintenance and rebalancing, do not use ride growth as the main proof of business quality.

## 11. What Was Unknowable

Vehicle durability, regulatory standardization and city-level density economics were still evolving and could differ materially by market.

## 12. Radar Verdict

**REJECT undifferentiated capital-heavy fleet replication; WATCH / START picks-and-shovels or controlled-environment variants with proven unit economics.**

### Lesson

> Usage is not unit economics. In asset-heavy networks, utilization must survive depreciation, operations, regulation and financing cost.
