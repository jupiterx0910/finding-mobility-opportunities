# Synthetic Example — Procurement / Supply Chain → Sourcing Infrastructure

> **Fictional operator archetype. Not based on any real user or private conversation.**

## Operator

A fictional procurement and quality professional has spent years evaluating suppliers, lead times, defect rates, MOQ and production constraints across one industrial category.

## Why this career is close to entrepreneurship

The role already accumulates:

- supplier maps;
- real price knowledge;
- quality variance knowledge;
- delivery performance knowledge;
- product-specification judgment;
- buyer-side understanding of what actually causes switching.

## Structural Need

Buyers repeatedly need reliable supply, not merely the lowest quoted price.

## Opening Change

A small operator can gain leverage when supplier markets are fragmented, digital sourcing makes discovery easier, and buyers increasingly outsource qualification, QC or low-volume sourcing.

## Concrete Offer

A narrow sourcing + quality-assurance service for one repeat-purchase category.

**Payer:** procurement / supply-chain / operations budget.

Possible deliverables:

- supplier shortlist;
- specification normalization;
- sample coordination;
- QC plan;
- delivery tracking;
- supplier scorecard.

## 30–60 Day Paid Pilot

1. Identify one category with repeat purchases and supplier variability.
2. Interview 10 qualified buyers.
3. Select one paid sourcing or QC problem.
4. Deliver against a measurable target: cost, defect rate, lead time or supplier reliability.
5. Track whether the same buyer returns for the next purchase cycle.

## Recurrence

The business becomes stronger when the buyer repeatedly relies on the operator for ongoing sourcing, supplier management or quality assurance.

## Power Pool

Weak position: generic sourcing labor.

Stronger position:

- proprietary supplier scorecards;
- trusted quality data;
- recurring buyer workflows;
- exclusive/hard-to-replicate supply relationships;
- category-specific private label or catalog.

## Assetization

`supplier knowledge → paid sourcing → recurring accounts → supplier scorecards → proprietary catalog → private label / data / procurement software`

## Kill Criteria

- customers can source equally well with minimal effort;
- margins collapse after QC/logistics costs;
- no repeat purchase cycle exists;
- advantage depends on employer-confidential supplier information.

## Verdict

**BUY A REAL OPTION** — validate repeat sourcing demand before taking inventory or product risk.
