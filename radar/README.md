# Monthly Opportunity Radar

The monthly radar is a **public decision log**, not a hot-sector list and not investment advice.

Each edition should make claims that can later be checked, downgraded or killed.

## Required fields

For every opportunity:

- **As of date**
- **Geography**
- **Stage:** S0–S6
- **Confidence:** High / Medium / Low
- **Structural need**
- **Opening change / why now**
- **Best-fit career/founder archetypes**
- **Payer hypothesis**
- **Power Pool hypothesis**
- **Leading indicators**
- **Confirmation indicators**
- **Kill criteria**
- **Unknowns**
- **Primary/authoritative sources**

## Evidence rules

- Prefer primary sources for policy, standards, product/platform changes and company announcements.
- Separate facts from Opportunity Radar inferences.
- Do not manufacture precision when evidence is incomplete.
- A policy announcement is not customer payment.
- A product launch is not retention.
- Funding is not demand.
- A leading signal is not a confirming signal.

## Update discipline

A later edition must explicitly state what changed:

- **UPGRADE** — stronger payment/retention/industry evidence;
- **UNCHANGED** — thesis still plausible but no major confirmation;
- **DOWNGRADE** — evidence weakened;
- **KILL** — a pre-written kill criterion triggered.

The purpose is to make the framework accountable to future evidence.
